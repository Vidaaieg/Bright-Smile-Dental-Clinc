how your visit works

Five steps from booking to follow-up, so you know exactly what to expect.

1

Book your appointment

Choose a service, date and time online, or call us. You get a confirmation straight away.

2

Consultation

We listen to your concern, look at your history and answer your questions before anything else happens.

3

Examination

A full check of your teeth and gums, with X-rays where they are needed, so nothing is missed.

4

Treatment

We explain the options and the cost, agree a plan with you, and only then begin the work.

5

Follow-up

Aftercare advice you can take home, and a review appointment to confirm everything healed well.
